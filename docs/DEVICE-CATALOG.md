# Device catalog

OmniCast reads the Android model and manufacturer from authorized ADB connections. The catalog matches exact model strings and explicitly listed aliases. Device pictures are downloaded from manufacturer pages on the first match and cached for offline use. A photo selected by the user takes priority. If no verified photo exists, OmniCast shows the manufacturer's logo where available and a device outline.

The photos depict the stated model in a manufacturer-selected color. Brand marks identify the connected hardware. The Sharp wordmark is sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Logo_of_the_Sharp_Corporation.svg). Additional logos are derived from [Simple Icons 16.38.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.38.0), with its CC0 dedication included alongside the images. Manufacturer marks and product images remain subject to their respective rights.

When adding a device to the catalog, verify the model code and product image against a manufacturer source. Avoid shared names that also identify a different physical handset.
